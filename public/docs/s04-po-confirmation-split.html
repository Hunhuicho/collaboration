MERP S04 INVESTIGATION FIRST REVIEW REQUESTED

PO Confirmation — What Ariba Allows

Before we settle any rule of our own we need to know what a supplier can actually do when confirming a PO in Ariba: what may they change, what may they split, and does it take effect immediately or does Purchasing have to act? Please answer Yes / No on the 7 items below and add a comment.

01

The case

One order line, one requested date — and a confirmation that comes back in two pieces.

PO issued 100 EA Item 00010 RDD 2026-03-10 Status Open
→
Supplier confirmation · schedule 1 50 EA Confirmed date 2026-03-10
Supplier confirmation · schedule 2 50 EA Confirmed date 2026-03-24
The requested date covered all 100. Here the supplier decides on its own to break the quantity apart and move half of it two weeks out. The requester agreed to one delivery and now there are two, with nobody on our side having approved the change.
02

What to ask Ariba DO THIS FIRST

Six questions in this order — each only matters if the one before it came back yes. Record what Ariba says under each one.

Why this comes first

We should not design a rule against behaviour we have not seen. Find out what the platform allows, watch it happen, then decide what MERP does.

1

Can a supplier change the PO at all when confirming?

Or is the confirmation only accept-as-issued and reject? A plain yes or no.

If nothing can be changed, questions 2 and 3 fall away.
Ariba's answer
2

If it can be changed — exactly which fields?

Quantity, delivery date, price, item, anything else. We want the actual list, not "the usual ones".

Decides what MERP has to be able to receive, and what we have to guard against.
Ariba's answer
3

After a change, is the PO confirmed straight away?

Or does Purchasing have to do something first — accept, approve, re-issue? If there is a step, what is it and where does the buyer see it?

This is the difference between the supplier changing our order and the supplier proposing a change.
Ariba's answer
4

Can the supplier split a line into several schedules?

One line of 100 confirmed as 50 on one date and 50 on another — different quantities, different dates, same PO line.

The case in section 01.
Ariba's answer
5

After a split, is the PO confirmed straight away?

The same question as 3, for the split case. Does it apply on its own, or does Purchasing confirm it again?

A split that applies silently is a different problem from one we can hold.
Ariba's answer
6

Show us — and show us what reaches MERP

A live walk-through of the 100 → 50 / 50 case on our own test account, plus the message that lands on our side and the fields carrying schedule quantity and date.

A demo settles in ten minutes what a specification argues about for weeks, and we cannot size the EDI work without seeing the payload.
Ariba's answer
→

Then we decide: follow it, or improve on it

With those answers in hand — does MERP simply do what Ariba does, or do we hold a stricter rule of our own? The rest of this document is our starting position for that conversation.

What a "yes" costs us

If Ariba allows the split and we accept it, MERP has to receive and store schedule-level data over EDI — schedule quantity and date per PO line, not just a confirmation flag. That reaches the PO tables, the receipt matching and the screens. It is development, not a configuration switch.
