PR cancellation (CN) — what should the trigger be?

CN is described only as "cancelled after approval for a later business reason". There is no rule for when it happens, who may do it, or what happens to the linked documents. Tell us how it should work in your own words.

  • Before approval — cancel, or withdraw and delete?
  • Once a PO exists — can the PR still be cancelled on its own?
  • Who has the authority, and is a reason mandatory?

05

PR / PO Modification

Each card takes its own Yes/No and comment: modification timing, reapproval and downstream control. PR first, then PO.

5-1. PR Modification

Changing an approved requisition and pushing the change through to the linked order.

SCOPE

PR Modification

Confirm the core rules for modifying an approved PR and propagating the change to the linked PO.

WORKING ASSUMPTION

Current proposal: allow only Quantity and RDD changes until the supplier registers a Delivery Note.

REVIEW REQUIRED

A PR quantity increase needs a policy decision before it can be built — it creates conflicting states between an already confirmed PO and the PR reapproval. Fallback for discussion: raise a new PR for the additional quantity.

01Reconfirm

1. Modification Window

Is PR modification allowed until the supplier registers a Delivery Note? The exact DN event that locks the PR and the linked PO has to be pinned down.

02Reconfirm

2. Editable Fields

Are Quantity and RDD (Requested Delivery Date) the only editable fields? Confirm whether any other field must stay editable after approval.

03Reconfirm

3. Quantity Decrease

For a decrease, may the system modify the PR, update the linked PO and resend the revised PO without another approval?

04Decision

4. Quantity Increase

An increase needs reapproval, but the supplier may ship or register a DN while approval is running. Should the increase be handled as a new PR for the additional quantity instead?

05Risk

5. Status during Reapproval

The existing PO may already be Supplier Confirmed while the increased quantity is under reapproval. Proposal: show the user 'PR Change Approval in Progress', keep PO Confirmed as the internal status of the previous PO version, and reset the revised PO to Open for re-confirmation after approval.

5-2. PO Modification

Changing an order that has already gone out to the supplier.

SOURCEDrafted, not received

The design file we received rendered only the PR Modification tab. The cards below are our draft of the PO side, written to mirror the PR rules — please correct them rather than confirm them.

SCOPE

PO Modification

Confirm when an issued PO may be changed, what may change, and how the supplier and the linked PR are kept in step.

WORKING ASSUMPTION

Draft proposal: allow Quantity and delivery date changes until the first Goods Receipt, with a revision number and a resend to the supplier.

REVIEW REQUIRED

A PO is approved at header level, so any change that crosses an approval threshold re-opens the whole order — not just the changed line. Where that line sits has to be decided before this can be built.

01Reconfirm

1. Modification Window

May an issued PO be changed until the first Goods Receipt is posted, or does the supplier's Delivery Note already lock it? A partially received PO — open lines only, or locked entirely?

02Reconfirm

2. Editable Fields

Draft: Quantity and delivery date are editable; unit price, currency, supplier and item are not — those need a new PO. Is that the right split?

03Reconfirm

3. Quantity Decrease

Draft: a decrease needs no reapproval — revise the PO, resend it and sync the linked PR. Does the supplier have to confirm the reduced quantity before it counts?

04Decision

4. Quantity Increase

An increase raises the order value, so header-level approval runs again. Should it instead be a new PO for the additional quantity, leaving the original untouched?

05Risk

5. Revision and Re-confirmation

Draft: a changed PO gets a revision number, is resent to the supplier, and its status returns to Open until the supplier confirms again. Is a Supplier Confirmed order really reset by any change, or only by ones that alter quantity or date?
